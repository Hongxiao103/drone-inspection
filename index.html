The research behind it.

My thesis asks how an AI assistant should support a drone operator who gives spoken requests after take-off.

Framework

4 ways to say a task (single or complex, clear or described target) × 3 ways to operate (on site, remote, confined space), each read through five kinds of interaction support: grounding, planning, approval, feedback, recovery.

Findings

843 records → 66 papers → 166 evidence records → 9 guidelines → 8 lo-fi screens. The same request needs different support depending on whether the operator can see the drone, whether space restricts it, and whether the task was interrupted.

In this installation

The four moments of the thesis scenario become one playable inspection: say what to inspect, let the drone act and watch, change the task mid-flight (“Unable to ascend” → tilt the camera), review the result. It demonstrates the logic; it does not test it.
